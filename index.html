About Us

At Kings Sports, we are building the leading basketball brand in Africa. We envision a future where basketball transcends boundaries and African talent shines on the global stage.

Our journey is fueled by an intense passion for the game, and our commitment extends beyond the court. We are actively amplifying the individual, social, and economic impact of youth sports.

We recognize and leverage trends, gaps, and opportunities within the basketball community, and use our convening power to promote African basketball by developing professional teams, promoting youth basketball, and establishing sports facilities.

Tiger Basketball Club

The Firdt South Sudan team to qualify for the Basketball Africa League (BAL).

Two-time unbeaten champions in the South Sudan Basketball Federation Premier League.

All in for the fans. #WeAreTigers

We made history in 2024 - and again in 2025 - by being the first South Sudan team to qualify for the Basketball Africa League (BAL). We also remained undefeated in two straight seasons in the South Sudaness Basketball League (2023-2024 & 2024-2025).

We are more than a winning basketball brand - we are on a mission to transform the landscape of the sport on the continent. We're dedicated to the growth of basketball in South Sudan, East Africa and beyond.

Kings Initiatives

Kings Sports, giving back to the community is a slam dunk. We bring professional-level coaching and resources right to their doorstept. Throught Youth developing programs, we're committed to nuturing the next generation of South Sudan basketball stars and fans. This includes rehabilitating community courts, providng mentorship, and offering top-notch coaching to help young atheletes reach their full potential.

Kings Men Street League Team

We build champions from the asphalt up. Our street Leauge teams cultivate raw talent honed on concreta courts, transforming streetballers into the next generation of stars. In our first year competing we proved it all on the court, taking home the Street leauge Championship!.

Kings Women Street Leauge team

We are commited to providing equall opportunities for young women to pursue their basketball ambitions. We established our women's street leauge team in 2023- the ladies qualified for playoffs and are gearing to top the league in 2027.

Court Rehabilation

We are actively building basketball courts in communities, providing a space for asfiring talents to home their skills. Our tansformative basketball camps are nuturing the next generation of basketball enhusiasts, ensuring a sustainable and thriving future for the sport.

Community Clinics

Growing stars on the court and in life. Our clinics brings basketball to underprivileged children in primary schools across Nairobi. We coach and mentor young minds, nuturing their love for the game while building a foundation for success on and off the court.

Dee Mentor

We've teamed up with Deementor to help young Ethiopian refugees in South Sudan find their place through the power of sports. They face challenges integrating into their new communities. Deementor provides crucial scholarship opportunities, allowing them to pursue their education alongside their athletic dreams.
