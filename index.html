About Us

At Kings Sports, we are building the leading basketball brand in Africa. We envision a future where basketball transcends boundaries and African talent shines on the global stage.

Our journey is fueled by intense passion for the game, and our commitment extends beyond the court. We are actively amplifying the individual, social, and economic impact of youth sports.

We recognize trends, gaps, and opportunities within the basketball community, using our convening power to promote African basketball by developing professional teams, promoting youth basketball, and establishing sports facilities.

Tiger Basketball Club

The first South Sudan team to qualify for the Basketball Africa League (BAL).

Two-time unbeaten champions in the South Sudan Basketball Federation Premier League.

All in for the fans. #WeAreTigers

We made history in 2024 and 2025 by being the first South Sudan team to qualify for the BAL. We remained undefeated in two straight seasons (2023-2024 & 2024-2025).

We are more than a winning basketball brand — we are transforming the landscape of the sport on the continent.

Nurturing Talent & Fostering Love for the Game

Kings Initiatives

Kings Sports believes giving back to the community is a slam dunk. We bring professional-level coaching and resources directly to young athletes. Through youth development programs, we're committed to nurturing the next generation of South Sudan basketball stars.

Kings Men Street League Team

We build champions from the asphalt up. Our street league teams cultivate raw talent, transforming streetballers into stars. In our first year we took home the Street League Championship!

Kings Women Street League Team

We are committed to providing equal opportunities for young women. Our women's team qualified for playoffs in 2023 and is aiming for the top in 2027.

Court Rehabilitation

We build and rehabilitate community courts, providing spaces for aspiring talents. Our transformative basketball camps nurture the next generation.

Community Clinics

Our clinics bring basketball to underprivileged children in Nairobi, coaching and mentoring young minds for success on and off the court.

Dee Mentor Partnership

Partnered with Dee Mentor to support young Ethiopian refugees in South Sudan through sports and education scholarships.
