About Us

At Kings Sports, we are building the leading basketball brand in Africa. We envision a future where basketball transcends boundaries and African talent shines on the global stage.

Our journey is fueled by an intense passion for the game, and our commitment extends beyond the court. We are actively amplifying the individual, social, and economic impact of youth sports.

We recognize and leverage trends, gaps, and opportunities within the basketball community, and use our convening power to promote African basketball by developing professional teams, promoting youth basketball, and establishing sports facilities.

Tiger Basketball Club

The First South Sudan team to qualify for the Basketball Africa League (BAL).

Two-time unbeaten champions in the South Sudan Basketball Federation Premier League.

All in for the fans. #WeAreTigers

We made history in 2024 and 2025 by being the first South Sudan team to qualify for the BAL. We remained undefeated in two straight seasons (2023-2024 & 2024-2025).

We are more than a winning basketball brand — we are on a mission to transform the landscape of the sport on the continent.

Nurturing Talent And Fostering A Love For The Game

Kings Initiatives

Kings Sports, giving back to the community is a slam dunk. We bring professional-level coaching and resources right to their doorstep. Through Youth Development programs, we're committed to nurturing the next generation of South Sudan basketball stars and fans. This includes rehabilitating community courts, providing mentorship, and offering top-notch coaching to help young athletes reach their full potential.

Kings Men Street League Team

We build champions from the asphalt up. Our street league teams cultivate raw talent honed on concrete courts, transforming streetballers into the next generation of stars. In our first year competing we proved it all on the court, taking home the Street League Championship!

Kings Women Street League Team

We are committed to providing equal opportunities for young women to pursue their basketball ambitions. We established our women's street league team in 2023 — the ladies qualified for playoffs and are gearing up to top the league in 2027.

Court Rehabilitation

We are actively building basketball courts in communities, providing a space for aspiring talents to hone their skills. Our transformative basketball camps are nurturing the next generation of basketball enthusiasts, ensuring a sustainable and thriving future for the sport.

Community Clinics

Growing stars on the court and in life. Our clinics bring basketball to underprivileged children in primary schools across the region. We coach and mentor young minds, nurturing their love for the game while building a foundation for success on and off the court.
